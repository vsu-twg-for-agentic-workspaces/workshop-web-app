    Privacy notice

    Before you register, please read how we handle your information. It takes about one minute.

    Who collects your information

    The facilitators of the TWG for Agentic Workspaces, Visayas State University, for the workshop shown above.

    What we collect

    • Your full name and institutional email, for your certificate
    • Your campus, office, position, whether you are faculty or staff, and how often you have used AI tools
    • Later, your workshop evaluation and attendance

    We do not ask for your birth date, home address, or ID numbers.

    Why we collect it

    • To prepare the participant list and track attendance
    • To prepare your certificate
    • To improve the workshop, using combined results only (never individual answers)

    Who can see it and where it is kept

    Only the three workshop facilitators. Your information is stored in a Google Sheet in a facilitator's Google account, and certificates are saved as PDF files in a Google Drive folder that only the facilitators can open. The app does not send you any email. It is kept for [retention period, to be confirmed by the facilitators], then deleted.

    Your rights

    Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the facilitators.
